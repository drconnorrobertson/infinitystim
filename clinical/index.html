Clinical Applications

Evidence-based electrostimulation therapy for a wide range of clinical conditions and patient populations.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Accelerate recovery following orthopedic, neurological, and general surgical procedures. NMES prevents muscle atrophy during immobilization while TENS provides effective post-operative pain management, reducing the need for pharmacological intervention.

Recommended modes: NMES, TENS, MICRO

Chronic Pain Management

Provide sustained, drug-free relief for patients with chronic pain conditions including arthritis, fibromyalgia, chronic low back pain, and neuropathic pain syndromes. The portable design enables consistent home-based therapy between clinical visits.

Recommended modes: TENS, MICRO

Sports Injury Recovery

Support athletes through injury recovery with targeted electrostimulation. MICRO reduces inflammation and promotes tissue repair, TENS manages pain, and NMES maintains conditioning during rest periods to minimize performance loss.

Recommended modes: MICRO, TENS, NMES

Wound Healing

Microcurrent therapy enhances the body's natural wound healing cascade by increasing ATP production, promoting fibroblast activity, and supporting collagen synthesis. Applicable for chronic wounds, surgical incisions, and ulcer management.

Recommended modes: MICRO

Muscle Re-education

Restore proper neuromuscular activation patterns following injury, surgery, or neurological events. NMES facilitates motor learning by producing controlled contractions that retrain the brain-muscle connection and rebuild functional movement.

Recommended modes: NMES

Edema Reduction

Manage swelling and fluid retention through electrostimulation-assisted lymphatic drainage. Microcurrent improves microcirculation while NMES muscle pumping action facilitates venous and lymphatic return in affected extremities.

Recommended modes: MICRO, NMES
