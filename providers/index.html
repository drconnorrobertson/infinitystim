Clinical Evidence

Electrostimulation therapy is supported by decades of peer-reviewed research. The three modalities in the Infinity Stim device are well-established in the clinical literature.

  • TENS for acute and chronic pain management
  • Microcurrent for tissue repair and wound healing
  • NMES for post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Combined modality treatment protocols

Patient Outcomes

Providers using the Infinity Stim report improved patient compliance and outcomes. The portable design encourages consistent home use between clinical visits, extending the therapeutic benefit of in-office treatments.

  • Improved treatment adherence through portability
  • Multi-modal therapy in a single device
  • Reduced reliance on pharmacological pain management
  • Patient empowerment through self-directed therapy

Billing & Coding Information

Electrostimulation therapy services may be billable under applicable CPT codes. Common codes associated with electrostimulation services include:

97032Electrical stimulation (manual)
97014Electrical stimulation (unattended)
97530Therapeutic activities
97110Therapeutic exercises

Always verify coding requirements with your payer and compliance team.
