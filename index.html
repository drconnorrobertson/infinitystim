Three Therapy Modes. Infinite Possibilities.

Each mode is engineered for specific clinical outcomes, giving you versatile treatment options in a single device.

MICRO

Microcurrent Therapy

Sub-sensory electrical currents that mirror the body's own bioelectric signals. Promotes tissue healing, reduces edema, and accelerates recovery at the cellular level.

TENS

Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation

Targeted pain relief through peripheral nerve stimulation. Effectively manages both acute and chronic pain conditions by modulating pain signal transmission.

NMES

Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation

Activates motor neurons to produce controlled muscle contractions. Ideal for muscle rehabilitation, preventing atrophy, and restoring neuromuscular function.
