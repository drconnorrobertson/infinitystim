Support Center

Everything you need to get the most from your Infinity Stim device.

User Guide Basics

Getting Started

Charge your Infinity Stim fully before first use via the USB charging cable. The LED indicator will show a solid light when fully charged. Power on using the main button and select your desired therapy mode.

Selecting Modes

Use the blue LED backlit mode button to cycle through MICRO, TENS, and NMES. The digital display will show the current mode and parameters. Adjust intensity using the up/down controls.

Treatment Duration

Set your desired treatment time using the timer function. Sessions typically range from 15 to 60 minutes depending on the mode and condition being treated. The device will automatically stop when the timer expires.

Electrode Placement

Proper electrode placement is essential for effective therapy. Follow these general guidelines:

  • Clean and dry the skin before applying electrodes
  • Place electrodes on or near the treatment area as directed by your healthcare provider
  • For TENS, electrodes are typically placed around or near the pain site
  • For NMES, place electrodes over the motor point of the target muscle
  • For MICRO, electrode placement varies by condition — follow clinical guidance
  • Ensure full electrode contact with the skin to avoid discomfort
  • Replace electrodes when adhesion decreases

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the battery last on a single charge?

Battery life varies by mode and intensity settings. Under typical use, expect multiple treatment sessions on a single charge. Charge the device using the included USB cable when the battery indicator shows low.

Can I use the device while it is charging?

For safety, we recommend completing charging before use. Disconnect the USB cable before beginning a treatment session.

How often should I replace the electrode pads?

Replace electrode pads when they no longer adhere firmly to the skin or when you notice reduced conductivity. With proper care and storage, pads typically last for multiple uses.

Is the Infinity Stim safe to use at home?

Yes. The Infinity Stim is designed for both clinical and home use. Always follow the user guide and your healthcare provider's recommendations for treatment parameters and electrode placement.

Who should not use the Infinity Stim?

Do not use the device if you have an implanted cardiac pacemaker, defibrillator, or other implanted electronic device. Do not apply electrodes over the carotid sinus, transthoracically, or on broken skin. Consult your healthcare provider before use during pregnancy or if you have epilepsy.

Troubleshooting

Device does not turn on

Ensure the device is charged. Connect to USB power and allow at least 30 minutes of charging. If the issue persists, contact support.

No sensation during treatment

Check that electrodes are properly connected and firmly adhered to the skin. Try increasing the intensity gradually. Ensure the correct mode is selected for your intended treatment.

Uneven or uncomfortable stimulation

This is usually caused by poor electrode contact. Remove electrodes, clean the skin, and reapply. Replace pads if they have lost adhesion.
