About Infinity Medical Devices

Advancing drug-free pain management and rehabilitation through innovative electrostimulation technology.

Our Mission

Infinity Medical Devices LLC was founded with a singular purpose: to make effective, drug-free pain management accessible to everyone. We believe that patients deserve non-pharmacological options for managing pain, accelerating healing, and restoring function.

The Infinity Stim represents our commitment to that mission — a professional-grade electrostimulation device that is portable enough for daily use and simple enough for anyone to operate.

Innovation Focus

Our engineering team has combined three of the most clinically validated electrostimulation modalities into a single compact device. By integrating MICRO, TENS, and NMES capabilities, we have eliminated the need for multiple devices while maintaining the clinical effectiveness that providers and patients demand.

Every design decision — from the USB rechargeable battery to the intuitive blue LED controls — reflects our focus on making advanced therapy technology practical for real-world use.

Quality & Compliance

Quality is fundamental to everything we do. Infinity Medical Devices LLC is committed to meeting the highest standards in medical device manufacturing, quality management, and regulatory compliance.

  • Rigorous quality management systems
  • Comprehensive product testing and validation
  • Ongoing post-market surveillance
  • Commitment to regulatory compliance
  • Continuous improvement processes

Our Values

  • Patient First — Every product decision starts with patient safety and therapeutic effectiveness.
  • Accessibility — Advanced therapy should be available to all who need it, not just those in clinical settings.
  • Innovation — We continuously improve our technology to deliver better outcomes.
  • Integrity — We are transparent about what our products can and cannot do.
