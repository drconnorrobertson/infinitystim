The Infinity Stim Device

Three clinically proven therapy modes engineered into one compact, portable electrostimulation device.

Device Overview

The Infinity Stim combines three distinct electrostimulation modalities into a single, easy-to-use portable device. Its compact round form factor, blue LED backlit buttons, and digital display make operation intuitive for both clinical and home settings.

Designed for patients and providers who demand versatility without complexity, the Infinity Stim delivers professional-grade therapy that travels with you.

Therapy Modes

  • MICRO (Microcurrent) — Sub-sensory currents that promote cellular healing, reduce edema, and support tissue repair. Operates at microampere levels to mirror the body's natural bioelectric currents.
  • TENS (Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation) — Modulates pain signal transmission through peripheral nerve stimulation. Clinically proven for both acute and chronic pain management.
  • NMES (Neuromuscular Electrical Stimulation) — Produces controlled muscle contractions through motor neuron activation. Prevents atrophy, supports muscle re-education, and aids post-surgical rehabilitation.

How It Works

1. Select Your Mode

Choose from MICRO, TENS, or NMES using the intuitive blue LED backlit buttons. The digital display confirms your selection and shows all active parameters.

2. Place Electrodes

Apply the standard snap-on electrode pads to the treatment area. The dual-channel output allows simultaneous treatment of multiple sites.

3. Adjust & Treat

Set your desired intensity and treatment duration. The device delivers precise electrical stimulation while you remain comfortable and mobile.
